Welcome to your programme

Here is how each daily session works.

  1. 1

    Warm Up

    Two short exercises to prepare your joints. Mark both complete before moving on.

  2. 2

    Baseline

    The camera measures your squat depth. Stand with your right side to the camera and do 3 squats to your comfortable maximum. This sets your personal target.

  3. 3

    Training

    Complete 5 squats to your daily target depth. The bar on screen shows how deep you are going in real time.

  4. 4

    Food

    Log whether you had protein with each meal today. Protein supports recovery.

  5. 5

    Progress

    See your session history and how your target is changing week by week.

Work through steps 1 to 3 in order each day. Steps 4 and 5 can be done at any time.

Mobility Prep

Complete both exercises before continuing.

1. Marching in Place

2 mins

March gently on the spot, lifting knees to a comfortable height. Swing arms naturally.

Why we do this A gentle warm-up raises your heart rate and loosens your joints before you squat. It is standard practice; direct trial evidence in older adults is limited.

2. Heel & Toe Raises

15 reps

Rise onto toes, hold briefly, then rock back onto heels. Use a wall for balance if needed.

Why we do this Rising onto your toes and rocking back onto your heels gently warms up your ankles and calves before you squat. It is standard practice; direct trial evidence in older adults is limited.

Set up safely

  • Stand next to a sturdy worktop or chair back that you can hold. Keep it within easy reach, on the side away from the camera.
  • Wear supportive, flat shoes.
  • Make sure the floor is clear, dry and not slippery. Move any loose rugs.
  • Have someone nearby for the first few times.
  • If you feel unsteady, hold on or stop.

Protein Check

As we get older, muscles need more protein to respond to exercise. Aim for about 25 to 30 g at each meal. If you have kidney disease or have been told to limit protein, follow your doctor's advice instead.
